# Submission Pack

Assembled at Phase 5 after the gates pass. Everything here is derived from the
draft — no new claims appear for the first time in the cover letter.

## Title page

- Title: method + task + distinguishing claim; no filler ("A novel…" adds
  nothing — the novelty should be legible without the word).
- Authors + affiliations + ORCID per venue; corresponding author; conflicts
  statement; funding statement; data availability statement.

## Abstract in the venue's format

Structured (Background/Methods/Results/Conclusions) vs. single-paragraph —
follow the guide exactly, including word count. The Results sentences carry
the primary numbers (matching `results_ledger.csv`). No citations in the
abstract unless the venue allows.

Keywords: 4-6, specific (modality, task, method family) rather than broad
("deep learning").

## Cover letter

Four short paragraphs, one page:

1. What this manuscript reports (2-3 sentences with the headline numbers).
2. Why it fits *this* journal specifically — cite the journal's scope, name
   1-2 recent related articles it published (verified).
3. What is new, in one sentence — aligned with the Introduction's contribution
   claims.
4. Standard declarations: original work, not under consideration elsewhere,
   all authors approved; conflicts/funding one-liners.

## Reproducibility statement (CS venues; increasingly journals too)

- Code/data availability (link, or "will be released upon acceptance" — only
  if true and the user confirmed).
- Compute: hardware, GPU-hours scale, seeds/variance reporting.
- For human data: ethics/consent basis, access conditions.

## Response-to-reviewers (when revisions come back)

- Point-by-point: quote the comment → answer → "changed in manuscript,
  Section X, paragraph Y".
- Never answer "we clarified" without showing the new text; never silently
  ignore a comment (decline with reasons if it's wrong).
- Re-run the Phase 5 gates on the revised draft — revisions are the most
  common place new placeholder/AI-tell text enters.
