# Writing Style

Same family as the repository's other writing skills; restated self-contained.

## Register and tense

- Methods/Results: past tense, "we did / we observed" — what happened.
- Discussion/Conclusion: present tense — what it means.
- No first-person plural inflation ("we believe" → say the evidence and hedge
  with "suggests").

## Prose rules

- Paragraph = one move (point → evidence → link to next). No bullet lists in
  Methods/Results/Discussion except genuinely enumerable protocol steps.
- Define every abbreviation at first use; keep a running list for the
  abbreviation table many venues require.
- Numbers: exact values in Results (from `results_ledger.csv`), rounded
  consistently; units always; dispersion/CI where available.
- Hedge to the evidence: "suggests/indicates/is consistent with" for
  interpretation; plain statements only for what was directly measured.

## Banned openings and filler (grep-gated in Phase 5)

- English: "Over the past decade/years, X has emerged…", "In recent years,",
  "It is worth noting that", "plays a crucial/pivotal role", "has garnered
  significant attention", "delves into", "a testament to".
- 中文: "近年来，随着……的飞速发展", "……日益受到广泛关注", "众所周知".

Open on the concrete: the task, the modality, the specific shortcoming.

## Cohesion

- Every table/figure is narrated: what it shows, what to notice, where it
  lands in the argument.
- Section transitions state the logical link, not "Next, we discuss…".
- The Abstract's numbers must equal the Results' numbers — the
  results_ledger reconciliation covers the abstract too.

## 中文语体

规范书面语；"本研究/本文"而非"我"；hedging 用"提示/在一定程度上/有待验证"；
术语首现给中英对照；标点用全角（数字与单位内除外）。
